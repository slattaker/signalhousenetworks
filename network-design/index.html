When network design helps

  • You are moving, expanding, or adding desks.
  • Calls drop or Wi-Fi slows down in certain rooms.
  • You need wired connections for computers, printers, or other equipment.
  • You want guest Wi-Fi separated from your business devices.

What we can help with

Office Wi-Fi coverage

Identify weak spots and plan access point placement around work areas, conference rooms, and walls that can weaken the signal.

Wired connections and switches

Plan which devices should be wired, how many network ports you need, and whether existing Ethernet cabling can be reused.

Business and guest network setup

Plan separate access for guests and business devices, and choose a layout that is easier to support when equipment changes.
